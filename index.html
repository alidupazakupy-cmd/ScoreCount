ScoreCount

Eurovision-style voting simulator: jury votes first, then the televote.

Settings

In jury grids every participant is also a voter and gives points from the scale to the others (e.g. 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 17, 20). Changing the scale clears the votes of the selected grid.

Voting grids

A televote tab works exactly like a jury grid (its own voters and scale), but in “Combined” its voters are hidden and only the televote result is shown next to the full jury grid. In a new televote tab nobody votes yet: add voters below.

Picking an entry that already received points from this voter swaps the two.

Voting grid

Odds – ratings

Tick the countries that belong in this list. Next to each flag type your ratings from 0 to 10 separated by commas – the average is calculated from them. When every ticked country is filled in, the percentages appear. Semi-final: chance to qualify, scaled so all countries together add up to Q × 100% (e.g. 10 qualifiers = 1000%). Grand final: winner odds inflated with a softmax of the averages (k adjustable; the better averages get disproportionately more), always adds up to 100%.

Random results

Generates points for every ticked country from its odds. The more deviation, the more the points can differ from the odds (upsets, swapped places). 0% = points follow the odds exactly. Works once all ratings are filled in.

Semi-final & Grand final draw

Add a list of countries (one per line). Press “Draw semi-finals” and they are split randomly into Semi-final 1 and Semi-final 2 (if the number is odd, Semi-final 1 gets one more). “Draw Grand final” puts all countries from the list in a random running order.
